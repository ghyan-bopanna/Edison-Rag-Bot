Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (often pronounced Azkaban):

Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban continues Harry’s journey into a wizarding world that is growing more complex and morally layered. The story begins during Harry’s summer holidays with the Dursleys, where tension quickly escalates after Aunt Marge insults Harry’s parents and is accidentally inflated by magic. Fearing punishment, Harry runs away and encounters a large black dog, which later becomes significant. He is rescued by the Knight Bus and taken to the Leaky Cauldron, where he learns alarming news: Sirius Black, a dangerous prisoner, has escaped from Azkaban, the wizard prison, and is believed to be hunting Harry.

Back at Hogwarts, security is tightened, and terrifying creatures called Dementors are stationed around the school to protect students. These beings feed on happiness and affect Harry more strongly than others, causing him to relive traumatic memories. Professor Lupin, the new Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher, helps Harry learn the Patronus Charm to defend himself against Dementors, forming an important bond with him in the process. Meanwhile, Ron’s pet rat Scabbers behaves strangely, and Hermione is overwhelmed by her unusually heavy academic workload, often appearing in multiple classes at once.

As the year progresses, Harry learns more about Sirius Black, who was once a close friend of his parents and is said to have betrayed them to Voldemort. Harry struggles with anger and a desire for revenge, believing Sirius is responsible for his parents’ deaths. The mystery deepens with repeated sightings of the black dog, believed to be the Grim, an omen of death.

The truth is finally revealed in the Shrieking Shack, where Harry, Ron, and Hermione confront Sirius Black. They discover that Sirius is actually innocent and that the real traitor is Peter Pettigrew, who has been hiding for years in the form of Ron’s rat, Scabbers. Pettigrew betrayed Harry’s parents and framed Sirius for his crimes. Professor Lupin, revealed to be a werewolf, confirms the story, explaining his past friendship with James Potter, Sirius Black, and Peter Pettigrew.

Tragically, Pettigrew escapes during a full moon transformation, preventing Sirius from clearing his name. Sirius is captured and sentenced to receive the Dementor’s Kiss, a fate worse than death. In a dramatic turn, Hermione reveals that she has been using a Time-Turner, allowing her and Harry to travel back in time. Together, they save Buckbeak the hippogriff and help Sirius escape on Buckbeak’s back, though his innocence remains officially unrecognized.

The novel ends on a hopeful yet bittersweet note. Sirius goes into hiding but offers Harry a sense of family and belonging he has never had before. Harry returns to the Dursleys with renewed confidence, knowing the truth about his parents and understanding that good and evil are not always as clear as they seem.
